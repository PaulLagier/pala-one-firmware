How to install

  1. Use Chrome, Edge, or Opera on a desktop computer. Safari, Firefox, and mobile browsers aren't supported.
  2. Plug your Pala One into your computer with a USB-C cable that carries data. If you are having issues, it may be that your cable is power-only. Try a different cable if the installer doesn't detect your board.
  3. Click an Install button above for your board and language. The browser will pop up a serial-port chooser.
  4. Pick your Pala One's serial port. On Windows it's usually not COM1. If you only see COM1, the board isn't plugged in or the cable doesn't carry data. On Mac / Linux, look for something like /dev/cu.usbmodem* or /dev/ttyACM*; skip Bluetooth and debug ports.
  5. Confirm Install in the dialog. Click Install, then Install again on the confirmation screen.

    ⚠️ Re-flashing an already-set-up device? Leave the Erase device checkbox unchecked — checking it wipes your books, settings, and saved Wi-Fi.

  6. Wait ~2 minutes while it flashes. You'll see a progress bar. Don't unplug.
  7. Click "Connect to Wi-Fi" when offered, then enter your home Wi-Fi credentials. This lets your Pala One join your network so you can upload books to it from any device on the same Wi-Fi. You can skip this, as there is a fallback when there is no Wi-Fi configured or you are away from your network.
  8. You're done! To upload books, navigate to "Upload" on the Pala One's menu and enter the URL shown on the device into your web browser.
